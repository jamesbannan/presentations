# Some fears misplaced, some partly right

::left::

### Misplaced

- Writing didn't end thinking: Plato wrote the warning down
- Paper didn't crumble: Trithemius had his book printed
- Photography didn't end painting
- Recording didn't end music-making

::right::

### Partly right

- Writing did change what we bother to remember
- Printing did, in time, retire the scribe
- Photography did take over much of the portrait's everyday work
- Making music at home did give ground to listening

# Generative AI doesn't invent our culture. It reflects it back at us.

# A change of scale and speed, not of kind

::left::

### Earlier technologies

- Copied, captured and distributed human work
- Fed back into culture over generations

::right::

### Generative AI

- Recombines human work into new artefacts
- Can feed back into culture, and its own training data, far faster
